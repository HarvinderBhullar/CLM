## Models (Hugging Face snapshot revisions)

| model | role | revision | precision |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Qwen/Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct` | image encoder (state) | `ebb281ec70b05090aa6165b016eac8ec08e71b17` | fp16 |
| `google/medsiglip-448` | image encoder (state); text tower for the zero-shot baseline | `9cea28a1a1195f665105faa6e8544c112fd960a4` | fp16 image / fp32 text |
| `Qwen/Qwen3-8B` | text encoder (action) | `b968826d9c46dd6066d109eabc6255188de91218` | bf16 |
| `Contrastive-LM/CLM-v0.1-8B` (`CLM_v0.1-8B.pt`) | released state/action heads | md5 `e7a1e1e25f8720da2072e3ba7957c866` | fp32 |

Qwen3-VL-8B was not used: it does not fit next to the system in 24 GB (the task spec picks the 4B model
for 24–32 GB machines). Consequently the released 4096-d state head cannot warm-start a vision head.
